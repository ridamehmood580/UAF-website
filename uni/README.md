# UAF Official Website

A modern, responsive, and interactive university website developed for the **University of Agriculture Faisalabad (UAF)**.

The project provides a centralized digital platform for students, faculty, researchers, applicants, and visitors to explore university information, academic programs, admissions, research, announcements, student resources, and other important services.

---

## 🌐 Project Overview

The UAF website is designed with a clean, modern, and responsive user interface that works across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

The application focuses on improving the digital experience of university users by providing important information through well-organized sections and interactive components.

### Main Objectives

- Provide easy access to university information
- Present academic programs and departments
- Display admissions and announcements
- Highlight research and innovation activities
- Provide useful student resources
- Improve communication between the university and students
- Provide interactive services and information
- Create a responsive and user-friendly digital experience

---

## ✨ Features

### 🏛️ University Information

- University introduction
- Vision and mission
- Vice Chancellor's message
- University announcements
- Important institutional information

### 🎓 Admissions

- Admission information
- Academic programs
- Admission-related announcements
- Important dates and schedules
- Student guidance

### 📚 Academic Departments

The website provides organized information about academic departments and programs offered by the university.

### 🔬 Research & Innovation

- Research and innovation section
- Research-related information
- Innovation initiatives
- Research highlights

### 👨‍🎓 Student Resources

A dedicated area for students containing useful resources and information.

### 📢 News & Announcements

- Latest university announcements
- Important notices
- Events and updates
- Highlighted information

### 🗓️ Schedule & Appointments

Interactive appointment scheduling functionality is provided through the appointment modal.

### 🤖 UAF Virtual Advisor

The application includes an interactive **UAF Virtual Advisor** interface designed to assist users with university-related questions and information.

### 🏫 Virtual Campus Tour

An interactive virtual campus tour section provides users with a digital way to explore the university environment.

### 📱 Responsive Design

The website is designed to work across:

- Desktop computers
- Laptops
- Tablets
- Mobile devices
